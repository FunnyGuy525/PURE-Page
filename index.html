About this reconstruction +

The long reconstruction uses PURE (DA3) Camera Head with 128-frame clips and 1-frame overlap. All 2,000 source frames appear once, in order, at 30 frames per second (about 1.25× the source speed). Only the viewing camera is smoothed; the reconstructed geometry is unchanged. The final reveal renders the complete filtered cloud without point subsampling. This is an offline temporal accumulation, not a live causal reconstruction. Some seams and moving-rider ghosting remain (7 of 15 seams meet the recorded geometry thresholds).

THE IDEA / 04

Unlabeled video.
Stronger dynamic 3D.

PURE uses unlabeled videos to post-train feed-forward 3D reconstruction models, improving accuracy in dynamic scenes. Monocular depth, dynamic masks and cross-frame matches provide training cues for depth ordering, local geometry and static cross-frame consistency.
